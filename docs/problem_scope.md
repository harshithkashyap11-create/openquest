# ClassPulse AI — Phase 1 Problem Scope

## MVP domain
- **Subject:** Mathematics
- **Topic:** Fractions (equivalence, comparison, addition and subtraction)
**Pilot size:** 30–40 synthetic learners

## Learning objectives
1. Identify numerator and denominator and explain their roles.
2. Recognize equivalent fractions.
3. Compare fractions using valid reasoning.
4. Find common denominators.
5. Add and subtract fractions accurately.
6. Explain the reasoning used to reach an answer.

## MVP learning cycle
1. Teacher creates a short assessment.
2. Students submit answers and brief explanations.
3. The system checks answers and inspects explanations.
4. The system assigns a provisional misconception label and confidence.
5. Ambiguous cases receive a diagnostic follow-up question.
6. Teacher reviews proposed groups.
7. Students receive targeted practice.
8. A reassessment measures progress.

## Non-goals for this MVP
- Automated high-stakes grading.
- Diagnosing a learner from one wrong answer alone.
- Supporting every subject or grade level.
- Replacing teacher judgement.
- Production identity management or real student data.

## Design principles
- Every diagnosis must retain the evidence that supports it.
- Low-confidence cases must be marked uncertain or sent for follow-up.
- Teachers approve groups and interventions before assignment.
- Synthetic data must be clearly distinguished from real classroom data.
