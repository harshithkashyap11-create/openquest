# ClassPulse AI: Feature Ideas

These are proposed additions and improvements, not a list of completed features.
Some build on existing ClassPulse capabilities, as noted below.

1. **Classroom Misconception Map** — visualize students and concepts that may need attention.
2. **Evidence behind diagnoses** — show the student's answer and supporting explanation beside the suggested misconception.
3. **Uncertainty and review queue** — highlight suggestions requiring teacher attention.
4. **Review and correct diagnoses** — make the existing teacher correction capability easy to find from the map.
5. **Review and approve groups** — surface the existing teacher group review workflow alongside suggested groups.
6. **Intervention action from the map** — let teachers choose support after reviewing a group.
7. **Needs attention today** — summarize pending diagnoses, uncertain cases, reviews, and students ready for reassessment.
8. **Student progress story** — connect the initial response, support, and reassessment results without asserting a causal improvement.
9. **Teacher onboarding guide** — explain diagnoses, corrections, groups, interventions, and reassessment.
10. **Teacher-control indicators** — distinguish AI suggestions from teacher-reviewed and approved decisions.
11. **Report a problem with AI output** — let teachers flag confusing, incorrect, or unsuitable suggestions.
12. **Accessible and reviewed language support** — improve accessibility and obtain human review of English, Hindi, and Telugu content.
13. **Student-data explanation** — explain what information is collected and how it is used.
14. **AI evaluation set** — assemble explanations labeled by educators for a held-out evaluation.
15. **AI evaluation report** — report correct, incorrect, and uncertain results by subject and misconception, alongside a simple baseline.
16. **System status view** — show application, database, and configured AI provider status.
17. **Clear error and retry messages** — show when an answer is saved and how to retry failed actions.
18. **Offline synchronization status** — make queued practice responses, completed syncs, and conflicts visible; practice queueing already exists.
19. **Support route** — provide a clear way to report problems or seek help.
20. **Reliable hosting and backups** — keep the application available without depending on a local laptop and protect stored data.
21. **Prepared demonstration** — set up a repeatable Fractions journey and a clearly labeled backup recording.
22. **Clear student journey** — show Assessment → Practice → Reassessment → Results with progress and saved-state messages.
23. **Unified student profile** — connect a student's work across Mathematics, Science, and English instead of separate topic-only class identities.
24. **Guided class setup and roster import** — allow CSV upload, error preview, and confirmation before importing students.
25. **Curriculum and objective tags** — organize questions, quizzes, and lessons by curriculum, grade, chapter, and learning objective.
26. **Stronger classroom security controls** — build on existing role and classroom checks with account recovery, access history, and data retention and deletion controls.
27. **Accessible assessment and practice flows** — review and improve keyboard access, screen-reader support, readable text, contrast, and status announcements.
28. **Synthetic demo reset** — restore a known demo dataset without touching real classroom records, paired with a readiness checklist.
29. **Stable deployment** — host the frontend, backend, and database with health checks, rather than relying on a temporary tunnel.
30. **School-level overview** — show authorized administrators aggregate activity and teacher review needs without ranking students by uncertain AI diagnoses.
31. **Teacher usefulness feedback** — collect optional feedback on diagnoses and interventions and aggregate it to guide improvements.

For a hackathon, prioritize the misconception map and a complete, reliable student-to-teacher learning journey, supported by honest educator evaluation. Hosting and backups, security, accessibility, and unified student records become especially important before a real school pilot.
